Understanding Fragrance Notes: Top, Heart & Base

A luxury perfume unfolds in three distinct layers over time. The Top Notes (such as citrus bergamot or pink pepper) create the intoxicating initial impression. The Heart Notes (like French velvet rose or lavender) form the core identity of the scent. Finally, the Base Notes (agarwood oud, golden amber, or white musk) anchor the fragrance to your skin for all-day projection.
