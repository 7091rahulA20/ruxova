Return & Refund Policy

Home → Return Policy

At RUXOVA PERFUMES, customer trust and satisfaction are our core values. We provide a structured 7-Day Replacement Guarantee for damaged, defective, or incorrectly delivered products. Please review our complete return policy terms below.

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

We offer a replacement within 7 days of delivery under the following eligible situations:

  • Damaged Product: Perfume bottle received broken, leaking, or cracked during transit.
  • Defective Atomizer/Spray: Spray mechanism or nozzle non-functional upon receipt.
  • Incorrect Product: Wrong perfume variant or size delivered compared to your invoice order.

2. Mandatory Packaging & Unboxing Requirements

To ensure fair verification and prevent fraudulent claims:

  • The product must be unused, in its original glass bottle, with original luxury outer box packaging and protective seals intact.
  • 48-Hour Damage Reporting Window: Transit damage or broken bottles must be reported within 48 hours of delivery along with clear unboxing photos or video evidence.

3. Hygiene Policy: Personal Scent Preference Exception

Note on Opened Perfumes: Due to personal care hygiene and product integrity standards, opened or sprayed perfume bottles cannot be returned simply because a customer dislikes the fragrance profile after opening. We encourage reviewing our detailed scent note profiles on product pages prior to purchase.

4. Free Reverse Pickup & Replacement Process

Once your replacement request is verified and approved, RUXOVA will schedule a reverse courier pickup from your delivery address within 24 to 48 hours at zero additional cost to you. A fresh replacement bottle will be dispatched upon pickup verification.

5. Out-of-Stock Replacements & Refund Timelines

If a replacement item is temporarily out of stock, a 100% full refund will be initiated:

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI, Cards, Netbanking): Refund will be credited back to your original source payment account within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds will be transferred directly via UPI ID or Bank NEFT provided by the customer within 3 to 5 business days.

6. How to Submit a Replacement Request

To initiate a replacement, please contact our support team with your Order ID and unboxing photos/video:

Frequently Asked Questions (Return FAQ)

Q1. How many days do I have to request a replacement?

You have 7 days from the date of delivery to submit a replacement request for eligible damaged or defective items.

Q2. What if my perfume bottle broke during transit?

Please take unboxing photos/video and notify us within 48 hours of delivery at ruxova47@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91-7808763348 for an immediate free replacement.

Q3. How long does a refund take for COD orders?

Once approved, COD refunds are processed via UPI or Bank NEFT transfer within 3 to 5 business days.
