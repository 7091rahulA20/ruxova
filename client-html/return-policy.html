Return & Refund Policy

Home → Return Policy

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

Customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We offer a 7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement Guarantee if your package arrives damaged, defective, or if an incorrect item was delivered.

2. Conditions for Return & Replacement

To be eligible for a return or replacement:

  • The item must be unused, in its original bottle, with original luxury box packaging and seal intact.
  • In case of transit damage or broken bottle, please share an unboxing video or photos within 48 hours of delivery to ruxova47@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91-7808763348.
  • Personal olfactory preference (disliking a fragrance scent profile after opening) is not eligible for return due to hygiene and safety standards for personal care products.

3. Reverse Pickup Process

Once your replacement request is approved, our courier partner will arrange a reverse pickup from your address within 24–48 hours at no additional cost to you.

4. Refund Policy & Timelines

- If a replacement product is out of stock, a 100% full refund will be initiated.
- Prepaid Orders (UPI/Cards/Netbanking): Refund will be credited back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
- COD Orders: Refunds will be sent via UPI ID or direct Bank NEFT transfer provided by the customer within 3 to 5 business days.

5. How to Initiate a Return

Please contact our Customer Care team with your Order ID and photo/video proof:
- Email: ruxova47@gmail.com
- WhatsApp: +91-7808763348
