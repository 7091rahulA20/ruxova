Shipping & Delivery Policy

Home → Shipping Policy

1. Shipping & Processing Timelines

At RUXOVA PERFUMES, all orders are processed with extreme care and elegance. Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched within 24 hours. Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST or on weekends/national holidays will be processed on the next working day.

2. Delivery Duration across India

We partner with premium express logistics providers (BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, and Xpressbees) to ensure your fragrance arrives safely:

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 2 to 4 Business Days
  • Rest of Tier-1 & Tier-2 Cities: 3 to 5 Business Days
  • Rest of India & Remote Locations: 4 to 7 Business Days

3. Shipping Charges & Free Shipping

- FREE Express Shipping on all orders above ₹499 across India.
- A flat shipping fee of ₹50 applies to orders below ₹499.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD)

We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across 25,000+ pin codes in India. A nominal COD handling charge of ₹40 is applicable for COD orders to cover courier collection service charges.

5. Order Tracking & Notifications

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and Email notification containing your Tracking ID (AWB) and a live tracking link. You can also track your order status directly from your RUXOVA account page.

6. Contact Support

If you experience any delay in delivery or have questions regarding your order shipment, please reach out to our dedicated support team:

- WhatsApp Support: +91-7808763348 (Mon–Sat, 10 AM – 7 PM IST)
