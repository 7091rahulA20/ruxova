Privacy Policy

Home → Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or place an order on RUXOVA PERFUMES, we collect personal information necessary to fulfill your order, including your name, shipping address, email address, phone number, and payment preferences.

2. Payment Security & Encryption

We prioritize your financial security. All online transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways using 256-bit SSL Encryption. RUXOVA does NOT store credit card details or bank passwords on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Processing and delivering your perfume orders across India.
  • Sending dispatch updates, tracking notifications, and order invoices.
  • Customer support inquiries and feedback resolution.
  • Improving web site experience and personalized fragrance recommendations.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies. We only share essential shipping details with our trusted courier logistics partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC) to ensure successful delivery.

5. Cookies Policy

We use cookies to maintain your active shopping cart, remember user login status, and analyze site performance to deliver a seamless shopping experience. You can disable cookies in your web browser settings if preferred.

6. Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding our privacy practices or to request account data deletion, email our Data Privacy Officer at ruxova47@gmail.com.
