Shipping & Delivery Policy

Home → Shipping Policy

At RUXOVA PERFUMES, we are committed to delivering your long-lasting fragrances safely, swiftly, and reliably across India. Every perfume bottle is packed in protective luxury box packaging to ensure safely scented arrivals.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines

We prepare all perfume orders promptly upon confirmation:

  • Same-Day Dispatch Window: Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days (Monday to Saturday) are dispatched within 24 hours.
  • Standard Processing: Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, on Sundays, or during national holidays are processed and handed to courier partners on the next business working day.

2. Delivery Timelines by Location in India

We partner with leading logistics networks to deliver online perfume orders across India:

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 2 to 4 Business Days
  • Tier-1 & Tier-2 Cities: 3 to 5 Business Days
  • Rest of India & Remote Locations: 4 to 7 Business Days

3. Shipping Charges & Free Shipping Threshold

We keep shipping fees simple and accessible for all perfume buyers:

  • FREE Express Shipping: Applicable on all orders total equal to or above ₹499.
  • Standard Shipping Fee: A flat shipping charge of ₹50 applies to orders under ₹499.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Options

Cash on Delivery is available across 25,000+ pin codes in India. A nominal COD handling fee of ₹40 is charged per order to cover courier cash collection services.

5. Order Tracking & Live Updates

Once your order leaves our facility, a SMS and Email notification containing your Tracking ID (AWB) and live tracking link will be sent to your registered contact details.

6. Logistics Delays & Address Accuracy

While we strictly maintain our dispatch commitments, transit delivery schedules may occasionally experience slight delays due to severe weather, regional logistics disruptions, or incomplete address details. Please ensure your shipping address, landmark, and mobile phone number are accurate during checkout.

7. Damaged Outer Transit Boxes

If your outer parcel box appears tampered or severely damaged upon delivery, please inspect the package in front of the courier agent or record an unboxing video before accepting it. For broken bottles or damaged contents, please review our RUXOVA Return Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions (Shipping FAQ)

Q1. How long does perfume delivery take across India?

Metro cities receive orders within 2 to 4 business days. Other cities and towns take between 3 to 7 business days depending on location.

Q2. Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available for my pin code?

Yes, COD is supported across 25,000+ pin codes in India with a flat ₹40 courier handling fee.

Q3. How can I get free shipping on RUXOVA Perfumes?

All orders above ₹499 automatically qualify for Free Express Shipping at checkout.
